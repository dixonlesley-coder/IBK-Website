## 7. Engineering philosophy (six pillars)

1. **Seismic Gap System** — AAC blocks with deliberate gap to columns/beams,
   filled with backer rod + sealant. Prevents hairline cracks (the most common
   defect in Indonesian homes).
2. **Zero Paint** — WPC panels + soft ceramic replace paint. No 3–5 year repaint
   cycle, no smell on site, no peeling/blistering/moisture damage.
3. **Zero Wood** — termites are Indonesia's most expensive hidden defect. Every
   door, panel, cabinet, ceiling is wood-free. Honeycomb aluminium, WPC, soft
   ceramic throughout.
4. **Double Beam Structure** — transfer beam distributes loads across two beams.
   Controls differential settlement on Jakarta coastal clay.
5. **Triple-Layer Waterproofing** — adhesive membrane + PU coating + integral
   admixture on all basement and wet areas. Most contractors apply one.
6. **Minimised Steel Structure** — where structure allows, RC concrete + post-
   tension replaces exposed steel. Steel rusts fast in Jakarta humidity.

## 9. Copy tone guide

**Indonesian** — write like you'd actually speak to a Jakarta developer or
homeowner. Avoid calque from English. Prefer:
- *"Kami" instead of *"IBK"* repeatedly (self-reference feels more natural).
- *"dibangun untuk bertahan"* rather than *"dibangun untuk ketahanan jangka
  panjang yang ekstensif"*.
- Direct numbers and facts — *"IDR 300 miliar dari 20 proyek"* — rather than
  *"portofolio proyek yang telah diselesaikan senilai IDR 300 miliar"*.
- Short sentences where the profile uses short sentences.
- Keep the profile's rhetorical moves: *"berapa biaya bangunan ini setelah
  selesai dibangun?"*, *"bukan biaya awal — biaya 20 tahun"*.

**English** — match the profile's voice: declarative, technical, confident.
Avoid hype adjectives (*state-of-the-art*, *world-class*, *premier*). Let the
numbers and specs speak. Use British spelling where the profile does
(*minimised*, *programme*, *specialise*).

**Don't use:**
- *"Bangun Sekali. Bertahan Dua Puluh Tahun."* (template relic — replace with
  the profile's *"Rancang. Bangun. Serahkan."* / *"Design. Build. Deliver."*)
- *"Biaya Kepemilikan 20 Tahun"* as a feature heading — the profile frames it
  as *"Berapa Biaya Gedung Anda Setelah Selesai Dibangun?"* (a question).
- Generic 9-item capability lists — use the six profile pillars.
- Fake project cards (*Villa Semarang*, *Ruko Bintaro*, *Kantor Tangerang*,
  *Rumah Bekasi*) — the profile lists the real 10 projects only.
